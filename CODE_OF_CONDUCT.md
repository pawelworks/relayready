# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive, assume good faith while evaluating evidence critically, respect privacy, and make room for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **Pavel Mihai Lucian** at
[lucian.pavel@pawelworks.com](mailto:lucian.pavel@pawelworks.com), with the subject
`RelayReady conduct report`. Share only the information needed to investigate;
do not disclose sensitive reports publicly. Reports will be handled discreetly,
but absolute confidentiality cannot be promised.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban participants when
necessary, and will explain decisions to affected people when safe and appropriate.
Reporters may ask for reconsideration through the same contact. Because the
project has one maintainer, there is currently no independent appeal or reporting
channel for complaints about that person. Establishing one is a governance gap,
not a service the project already provides.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
